# Sprint 002: retrospective

> The same anchor for every check makes every check comparable.

## What worked

### Asking planning to own the rule

Once the rule was theirs, every later discussion about what the floor should see started from it.

### Reusing the order anchor for the documents

The first list of documents came from a manual export by description. Anchoring on orders gave a
criterion anyone could rerun, and it surfaced the 16 products with no raw materials registered.

## What could improve

### The formula's line was misleading

Many formulas have no line set. Scoping by the formula's line would have dropped them. The order's
line was the right field, and it took a wrong first cut to see it.

### Parts with the same description

Three pairs of distinct, active parts share a description in the integrated lines. On the terminal,
an operator can't tell them apart. It was flagged as a risk, not fixed, and it still depends on
planning renaming them.

## What changed

- Scope is always decided by the order's fields.
- Same-description pairs are reported in every readiness check.
