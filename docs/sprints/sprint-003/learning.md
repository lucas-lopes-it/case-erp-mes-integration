# Sprint 003: learning

## Words like "everything" are scope decisions in disguise

### What happened

I promised to cover "everything" in the documents for the terminal. Each team heard its own version.
Nobody was wrong; the word just didn't carry a scope.

### Why this matters for product people

Scope is usually negotiated on the big items and lost on the small words: all, everything, done,
complete. Each one invites the listener to fill in the boundary. In an integration, where several
teams depend on the same delivery, the boundary each one fills in is different.

### How to apply it

1. When you hear yourself say "everything" or "all", stop and list what is in.
2. List, just as explicitly, what is out and who owns it.
3. Send both lists in the same message as the promise.

### Exercise

Search your last week of messages for "everything", "all of it" or "done". For each one, write the
scope you meant. Then ask one person who read it what they understood.
