# Sprint 003: retrospective

> A promise without a scope is two promises.

## What worked

### Saying the scope back in writing

One message with "this is what I'm covering, this is what I'm not" ended a misunderstanding that
could have cost trust with IT and planning.

### Asking the vendor before building around them

The best solution for the photos was not a new process on our side. It was a capability the MES might
already have.

## What could improve

### "Everything" should never have been said

I said I would take care of "everything". To me, that meant the ERP documents. To IT and planning, it
also meant the photos. The misunderstanding was mine to prevent.

## What changed

- Promises come with a written scope in the same message.
